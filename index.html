Desk 4 · Floor manual

How I run a floor

OPERATING PRINCIPLES

01

Document it while it is hot

Case notes written during the call, not after the shift. That is what makes a record audit-ready instead of approximately true.

02

Coach before you correct

Live call monitoring exists to catch the coachable moment early. Performance plans are a last resort, and people come off them.

03

Build the thing once

Onboarding guides, scripts and SOPs mean the next new hire does not cost the team a week of repeated answers.

04

Account for every asset

If it can be borrowed, it gets logged: borrower, time out, time back, condition. Gaps in the log become losses on the balance sheet.

05

Escalate to the right place

Legal, technical, logistics, line manager. Knowing the route is half of resolution speed.

06

Protect the schedule

Coverage gaps are a morale problem before they are a service level problem. Fix the roster, and the numbers follow.
